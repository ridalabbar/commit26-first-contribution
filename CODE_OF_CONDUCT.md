# Code of Conduct

This repository is a welcoming learning space.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and patient.
- Welcome beginner questions.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Do not use insulting, discriminatory, or harassing language.

Unacceptable behaviour may result in comments, issues, or contributions being removed. Serious or repeated behaviour may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from the repository.

If something makes you uncomfortable, contact the repository owner privately through an appropriate channel rather than posting personal details publicly.
